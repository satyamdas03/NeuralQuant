6. Objections and rebuttals

"We can build this ourselves."

NeuralQuant represents 24–37 senior engineer-months of live, scar-tissue-hardened work. Rebuilding the dual-market data pipeline, voice stack, and adversarial agent orchestration typically takes 18–24 months and a comparable bug list. Acquisition collapses that to a 30-day API integration.

"It is pre-revenue."

That is the point. There is no inherited churn, no legacy pricing, and no support debt. Payments infrastructure (Stripe/PayPal) is already wired. The buyer captures 100% of monetisation upside while buying a de-risked production platform.

"StockEdge already has AI."

StockEdge's AI is chart-pattern detection and momentum scoring — useful, but narrow. It has no conversational reasoning, no bear-case challenge, no backtest engine, and no voice. Those are the features users are beginning to expect from AI-native competitors like Trendlyne and VestAI.

"We don't need US stocks."

US coverage is not the main ask — it is the differentiator. It gives StockEdge's Premium/Pro tiers a feature no India-only competitor has, and creates a cross-market education narrative for Elearnmarkets and Face2Face content.
